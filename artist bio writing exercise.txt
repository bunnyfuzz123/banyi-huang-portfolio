artist bio writing exercise






I make worlds. 
I use 3d modeling as a basis to create worlds. 
I am moved by visual experiences and experimentation
I make performances with my body
I enjoy taking existing systems and mashing them together, 
such as reanimating myth through my perspective and experience as a diasporic person, immigrant, and desiring body. 
a deity, a certain texture, simulating movement, can become a container that holds my emotions at a given time. 

i have made 3d printed sculptures that encapsulate forms as ritualistic gestures. 

i make virtual creatures, strange bodies, and affective environments. 
	i am not the originator of strangeness. i am simply one who uncovers the strangeness and uncategorizabiltiy of things. 

I like to inhabit and move between different scales and registers of experience and meaning. 

我喜欢研究 how things are put together，接着把部分拆开，重新组合。see what new configurations can create 
this includes cosmological systems in traditional Chinese understandings of the world, CGI pipeline, a durational live performance. 

i keep returning to the same thing: what does it feel like to belong/inhabit and be outside of yourself at the same time? 
as systems increasingly become more complicated, opaque, and black box, it comforts me to return to low level registers that constitute systems: counting one, two, three in breathing meditation, my own liveliness that out lives the projection of meaning, my own skin that eventually becomes the texture of a virtual environment.
